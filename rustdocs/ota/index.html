Zafu/Zigner firmware OTA wire contract — device side.

Implements the frozen wire contract (docs/design/zafu-ota-wire-freeze.md): strict canonical CBOR (RFC 8949), Ed25519 verify/sign over domain-tagged canonical forms, stream verification, A/B staging (pure logic), and the device-signed result/status encoders.

Everything here is pure and unit-testable; the Android exposure lives in the signer crate (FFI) and ModuleSlotStore-style Kotlin adapters.
